# Jester Dev'il

Jester Dev'il is his stage name, however that is the name everyone knows him by.

## Personality
Jester Dev'il is super sweet to a near toxic degree and makes fun of most people (Sometimes unknowingly). He is obsessed with his partnear and never missing a beat to praise them. He changes personalities through changing masks.

He is married to/has a romanic relationship with King Devana.

## Unique Quality
Jester Dev'il is affected by somehing unatural and can't die. However, while not being able to die permenatly he will still die and has dug out of graves constantly.

## Masks
- Lamb is one of Jester Dev'il's many masks and is very stern on vices. During younger years found "purity" through the murdering of smaller animals, though get upset if other bring it up. He is now very into religion and is a main member of the six gates keeping them as a written religion.
