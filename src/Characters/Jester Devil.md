# Jester Dev'il

Jester Dev'il is his stage name; that is the name everyone knows him by.

## Personality
Jester Dev'il is super sweet to a near-toxic degree and makes fun of most people (sometimes unknowingly). He is obsessed with his partner and never misses a beat to praise them. He changes personalities through changing masks.

He is married to/has a romantic relationship with King Devana.

## Unique Quality
Jester Dev'il is affected by something unnatural and can't die. However, while not being able to die permanently, he will still die and has dug himself out of graves constantly.

## Masks
- Lamb is one of Jester Dev'il's many masks and is very stern on vices. During his younger years, he found "purity" through the murdering of smaller animals, though he gets upset if others bring it up. He is now very into religion and is a main member of the Six Gates, keeping them as a written religion.
