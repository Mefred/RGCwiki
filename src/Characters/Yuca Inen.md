# Yuca Inen

## Backstory
Yuca Inen is an elf from a far away icy tundra. There, a small community of elves have existed since the beginning of time. They kept to themselves, and have ambitions. They see no purpose in doing so, they remain isolated to one Russian village. Yuca Inen however was a horrid alchoholic. They partook in vodka almost daily, drowning themselves in their own despair. They were bored by life in the icy tundra. Eventually, for their misanthropic ways, they were banished from the village, and they were happy to go, until they realized they lost their supply of booze. They ventured about the wilderness for 50 years. They were not very productive in their search for a new home. They frequently ate rotten fruit in hopes of getting off of it. In one drunken state, on a rainy night, they became aware of the Rainism Gods, and sought to serve them. They were awed by the grandeur of the God's focus, their focus on fate. As they traveled on, they felt more and more the feeling of connection to the Rainist Gods. They felt a part of something much bigger than them everytime it would rain. Eventually, they found the remains of what was once a kingdom. Their parents had spoken of a kingdom of humans far away, yet no kingdom existed there. As soon as they arrived, they immediately delighted in the presence of booze and a new type of pass time, smokes, that there was, and they too joyed in the presence of those who too knew of the Deity's that held power when water poured. They do not understand Human culture, they speak very little English, and they are pouncing on the opportunity to drink alchohol again.

## Beliefs
Yuca Inen believes that fate is already decided. Becuase of this, she will take just about any job. If someone gives a job and offers payment in the forms of simple things as alcohol she sees it as a job placed before by fate. Declining would be denying the path created by fate and rejecting the teachings of rainism. Yuca Inen still expects a honest agreement between both parties. If one lies, cheats, or beaks their word, she believes they are trying the act against the order of established by fate and that she must restore balance, whether through punishment, or whatever actions she believes fate demands.

## Unique Quality
Yuca Inen is colorblind with deuteranopia (Green blind/has no green color cone).
