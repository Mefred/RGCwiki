# Gate 4

Gate 4 saw the land above, wishing to bring a beautiful side to it.

## Acts
Gate 4 used their own tears to make rain, bringing Gate 3 to the land (the beautiful side), and watched things blossom from it.
Gate 4 makes small gifts, rainbows, rain, the ocean tide, and the moon, together with Gate 3, as gifts and actions for each other.

## Beliefs
Despite being locked away, Gate 4 is the healthiest and sees humans normally.
