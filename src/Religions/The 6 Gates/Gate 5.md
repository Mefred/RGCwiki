# Gate 5

Gate 5 is pure evil. They are aware of how the moral system works for mortals. While they are incapable of pleasure, they have the closest thing to it with their job.

## Control
Gate 5 controls the "final resting place"; as when the world resets, consuming the world isn't enough energy, so instead they use vessels. (If there is not enough energy, Gate 5 eats humans' thoughts, souls, and entire being as fuel)
