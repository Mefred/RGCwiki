# Gate 3

Gate 3's innocence was stained by the ocean, their gate being at the bottom of it. Seeing how it moved, not hurtful but natural even in its wrath, slowly twisted it behind the gate to be harsher.

## Acts
After drowning and being underwater, it eventually created the drowned and the monuments.
Gate 3 makes small gifts, rainbows, rain, the ocean tide, and the moon, together with Gate 4, as gifts and actions for each other.
Gate 3 uses the ocean to drown, drive mad, and create creatures, all from the depths.

## Beliefs
Gate 3 uses the ocean to drown, drive mad, and create creatures, all from the depths, because they see it as a stain on Gate 4's gifts.
