# Gate 1

Gate 1 is aware of humans and vessels and absolutely hates them for the misery they go through.

## Acts
To let them survive and live abundantly they are one of the few who actively partake in draining vessels for pleasure and energy. They make vessels hyper aware to put them in as much agony as possible. (To the deterement of one other gate).

### How
They use messages, mobs, and sometimes events to inact there will.

## Apperence
They appear as a child in constant panic, with flashes of stone cold awareness of the act they are playing.
